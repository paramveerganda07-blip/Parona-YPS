YPS Patiala Event Portal

78th Annual Sports Day & Events • Privacy Policy & Support

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Welcome to the YPS Patiala Event Portal App ("Parona X YPS"). This application is provided specifically for students, parents, faculty, alumni, and distinguished guests attending the 78th Annual Sports Day and school campus events at Yadavindra Public School, Patiala.

1. Information We Access & Collect

This event portal is designed for attendee convenience and privacy:

  • Event Schedules & Itinerary: Publicly broadcasts event rosters, sports timings, and house points for attendees.
  • Parking Pass Registration: If you register a vehicle for campus parking, your vehicle number is used solely to generate a single-use digital parking ticket for security gate clearance.
  • Feedback: Any feedback submitted through the app is used purely by the organizing committee to improve school event arrangements.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Internet Access: To retrieve live event timelines, race results, house points, and emergency medical contacts.
  • Camera (Optional): Used only if you scan parking QR codes upon arriving at the campus gates.

3. Data Protection & Security

All data transmitted between the app and the server is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal data to third parties.

4. Children’s Privacy

The app displays sporting achievements and event participation in accordance with school-authorized athletic activities. It does not collect behavioral advertising data or track student locations.
